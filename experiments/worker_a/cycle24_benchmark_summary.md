# Worker A Cycle 24 benchmark

A retrospective test of 167 gold-futures daily closes compared four seven-session prediction rules. On 11 nonoverlapping 2026 observations, persistence had MAE 174.57 USD/oz, damped momentum 181.78, full momentum 214.10, and weak mean reversion 171.46. Mean reversion's small advantage is not robust. This is not a test of the separate spot-gold forecast. Full methodology and locally tested code are preserved in the cycle artifact.
